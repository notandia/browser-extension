About Notandia

Notandia, previously distributed as MDPI Filter, adds user-controlled publisher watchlists and explainable post-publication signals while you search for and read scholarly literature.

Interpretation: a publisher watchlist match identifies evidence selected in your settings. It is not a quality score or a claim that every article from that publisher is unreliable.

Notandia is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by MDPI AG, Frontiers Media SA, Crossref, Retraction Watch, NCBI, browser vendors, or any publisher or data provider.

Features

  • Includes independently configurable MDPI and Frontiers profiles, enabled and highlighted by default.
  • Supports context-only, badge, highlight, dim, and hide behavior per publisher.
  • Allows validated custom publisher profiles using domains and DOI prefixes.
  • Integrates current-article, reference-list, and supported search-result publisher context.
  • Optionally checks DOI metadata for formal retractions, concerns, corrections, reinstatements, withdrawals, and duplicate-publication findings.
  • Checks both direct Crossref records and reverse update relationships for separately registered notices.
  • Shows provenance, coverage, deferred checks, and unresolved identifiers without assigning a hidden quality score.
  • Keeps integrity lookups off by default and supports a zero-network configuration.
