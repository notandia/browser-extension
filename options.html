Publisher watchlist

Choose which publishers Notandia identifies and how matching articles, references, and search results are treated. MDPI and Frontiers are enabled and highlighted by default, but both can be disabled or changed independently.

Important: a watchlist match identifies a publisher selected in your settings. It is not a reliability score and does not claim that every article from that publisher is low quality.
